      Studio

      A small studio in an old ropeworks

      VERDURA is a practice of architects, a model maker and a landscape designer working from a restored ropeworks by the harbour. We take on a few projects at a time and stay with each one until it is built.

      • Build less

        The first question on every project is whether the building already exists and only needs mending.

      • Build with what grows

        Timber, straw and earth from as close to the site as we can find them.

      • Design for repair

        Pegged joints, screwed fixings and exposed services, so the next person can take it apart.

      • Let the site lead

        We walk it at different hours and seasons before drawing a single line of the plan.
